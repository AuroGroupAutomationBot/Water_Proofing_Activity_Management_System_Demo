🚫 1. Stop Work Notification — Entire Tower Freeze (BRD §11.4)

Structural or systemic hazards trigger an immediate blanket Entire Tower Freeze. All active stages across all floors and flats in the tower are halted until joint Quality Head and PM clearance.

⚠ 2. 10-Minute NCR — Floor Worksite Freeze (BRD §11)

Quality Head endorsement halts works across the entire floor slab (Floor Freeze) with a 10-minute escalation countdown timer until compliance submission is inspected and accepted by QC.

👁 3. Quality Observations — Flat Freeze (BRD §12)

Bilateral fast-track resolution between QC and site engineers for unit defects. Freezes the specific flat (Flat Freeze) while adjacent units proceed uninterrupted until photographic closeout.

📐 4. 1:100 Slope & 300mm Upturn Mandate

Minimum 1:100 protection screed gradient directed toward drainage outlets, with 300mm continuous membrane upturn on perimeter walls to guarantee comprehensive splash zone protection.
